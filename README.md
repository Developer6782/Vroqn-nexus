# Vroqn Nexus

An AI-powered learning workspace for school students.

Vroqn Nexus brings learning, practice, notes, coding, mock exams, competitions, communities and progress tracking into one connected workspace — so students don't have to use different apps for every part of their study.

```text
LEARN → PRACTISE → BUILD → TEST → ANALYSE → IMPROVE

Built mobile-first with a mechanical-dark interface, cyan accents, clear typography and a responsive experience for phones, tablets and desktops.


---

What Vroqn Nexus Helps Students Do

Area	What it does

AI Tutor	Ask questions, understand difficult concepts, get step-by-step explanations, examples, simpler explanations and follow-up help.
Practice	Select a subject, chapter, difficulty and question type, then practise questions one by one with explanations and working.
Mock Exams	Take timed mock exams, navigate questions, mark questions for review and receive detailed performance analysis afterwards.
Notes	Create and organise notes, upload handwritten notes, PDFs or text and turn them into useful study material.
Code Lab	Practise JavaScript, Python and HTML/CSS/JS, preview projects and use AI assistance to understand and improve code.
Arena	Participate in competitive mock exams, view rankings and analyse performance after competitions.
Communities	Join or create student learning communities, discuss doubts, share resources, participate in challenges and study together.
Private Chat	Communicate privately with other students with privacy and safety controls.
Learning Activity	Understand your own study activity, practice progress, exam performance, strong topics and weak topics.
Search & Discovery	Find relevant learning content, communities and student resources.
Student Profile	Maintain a student profile and control what information is shared with others.

The Learning Experience

Vroqn is built around a simple learning cycle:

UNDERSTAND
    ↓
PRACTISE
    ↓
BUILD
    ↓
TEST
    ↓
ANALYSE
    ↓
IMPROVE
    ↓
LEARN AGAIN

Instead of being only an AI chat box, Vroqn connects different parts of the student's learning journey together.

A student can learn a concept, practise it, take a mock exam, identify weak areas and continue practising those areas.


---

AI Tutor

The AI Tutor helps students learn concepts interactively.

Students can:

Ask questions about difficult topics.

Get explanations in simpler language.

Ask for examples.

Get step-by-step solutions.

Ask why something works.

Ask to be tested.

Continue with follow-up questions.

Get help while studying notes and learning material.


The goal is not only to provide an answer, but to help students understand the concept behind it.


---

Practice

The Practice section allows students to create personalised practice sessions.

Students can select:

Subject

Chapter

Difficulty

Question type

Number of questions


Questions are presented one at a time so students can focus on solving.

After answering, students can see explanations and working where appropriate.


---

Mock Exams

Vroqn provides a dedicated mock-exam experience for self-practice.

Students can:

Take timed papers.

Navigate between questions.

Mark questions for review.

Track unanswered questions.

Submit their paper.

Review their performance afterwards.


The result experience focuses on more than just marks.

Students can see:

Score

Accuracy

Correct and incorrect answers

Time performance

Strong topics

Weak topics

Areas that need revision

Suggestions for further practice


This turns an exam result into a learning feedback loop.


---

Vroqn Arena

Vroqn Arena is the competitive learning section of Vroqn.

Students can participate in competitive mock exams and compare their performance with other participants.

Arena provides:

Scheduled competitions

Timed exam experience

Question navigation

Autosave

Answer review

Scoring

Ranking

Percentile

Subject analysis

Topic analysis

Difficulty analysis

Time analysis

Post-exam AI analysis

Practice suggestions based on weak areas


Arena competitions are independent Vroqn mock competitions and do not represent official JEE or NEET rankings.
